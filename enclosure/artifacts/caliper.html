Mukklet caliper guide

The sizes the Mukklet case needs. The ESP32 mini was measured on 2026-10-01 and the TFT on 2026-09-29. Nothing is left to measure. All values in millimetres.

How to hold the ESP32 mini

Every top-view step shows the board metal can towards you, USB socket at the bottom, antenna at the top, as in your first photo. "Left" is the side with the RST button.

  • Measure it bare, before soldering the pins.
  • Always edge to edge. Nothing is measured from the centre of a hole.
  • The numbers in grey are what I'd assume without you (the seller's size, or a guess). Yours replace them.

How to hold the screen

Every TFT step shows the board from the front (glass towards you), pin header at the bottom, the way it's mounted in the case. "Top" is the edge without pins: measure from the tops of the two ears (the corners with the holes), not the notch between them where the flat cable wraps round. "Left" is your left with the glass facing you.

  • Already measured, nothing to do: the KY-040 board, knob cap and shaft position (same part as the desk display).
